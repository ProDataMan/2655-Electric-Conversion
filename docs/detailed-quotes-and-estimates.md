## High-Power Options for 2655 (Planing + Displacement)

### Evoy Systems (Norwegian, high performance)
- **Storm 300+ Outboard** (225 kW / ~300 hp continuous, 450 kW peak, ~385 kg motor):
  - Motor only: ~€74,900 (configurator signal, Aug 2026).
  - Full system with batteries: Historical packages ~€144,700+ for ~126 kWh configurations; higher for larger banks.
  - Batteries: 63 / 126 / 189 kWh liquid-cooled LFP options (IP67, ~3000 cycles @ 70% DOD). CE or DNV certified variants.
  - Charging: 20 kW AC standard; DC fast up to 200 kW (1C limited).
  - Ideal for 25-35 ft single install. Strong planing performance demonstrated (e.g., >50 kt on lighter hulls, ~25 nm at planing speeds).
  - Contact: https://config.evoy.no/ or dealers; sales via configurator form.

- **Breeze 120+** (90 kW / ~120 hp continuous, 137 kW peak, ~240 kg):
  - Outboard: ~€39,900 (configurator).
  - Inboard G2 (newer, with Taiga NMC batteries): From €34,990 motor + €17,490 for dual 24 kWh packs (48 kWh usable). Fast charge 10-80% in ~21 min. Range estimates 20-40 nm planing, longer displacement.
  - Better weight/price entry for planing capability on the 2655.

### RAD Propulsion (UK, integrated steering, commercial focus)
- **RAD 40** (40 kW continuous, ~55-60 hp thrust equivalent, axial-flux, 98-104 kg):
  - Engine historically from ~£28,000.
  - Batteries: 21 kWh (~130 kg) and 61 kWh (~350 kg) modules; Power Console packages 21/42 kWh.
  - Finance example: Complete package from ~£995/month (3-5 years).
  - 400V architecture, CCS fast charging. Integrated 180° steering.
  - Suitable for twin setup on 2655 for higher total power.

- **RAD 120** (120 kW continuous, 175 kg, launched Nov 2025):
  - Expected commercial availability late 2026. Min recommended battery 61 kWh (preferred 122 kWh+), up to 244 kWh linked.
  - Same architecture as RAD 40. Strong candidate once available.
  - Contact: https://radpropulsion.com/contact-support/ for quote/demo.

### Vision Marine Technologies (Canada, strong pontoon integration)
- **E-Motion 180E** (~135 kW / 180 hp continuous at prop):
  - Full electric pontoons: V24 starting ~$100,000–$120,000 USD (43 or 86 kWh options, 40-90 nm range claims at low speed).
  - V30 ~$140,000–$160,000.
  - P Powerpack available for pontoon integration.
  - Onboard charger compatible with 120/240V shore power.
  - Good for rental fleet standardization; proven on pontoons.

### ePropulsion (Widely available, modular)
- **X40 Outboard** (40 kW, 96V eSSA): $19,500 motor.
- **G-Series Batteries** (LFP):
  - G102-100 (~10.2 kWh): ~$5,300–$5,800.
  - G102-230 (~23.5 kWh): ~$11,000–$12,000.
- Example system: X40 + 4× G102-100 (~41 kWh) ~$36,000 components (ex controls/prop).
- Higher-power pods (12/20 kW eSSA) available for lower-power or dual setups. Good for initial testing or pontoon.

### Bosch Engineering EDM 160
- 120 kW / 160 hp continuous, 140 kW peak, ~180 kg integrated inboard (motor + inverter + charger + cooling).
- Open seawater cooling, up to 94% efficiency. Twin capable.
- Price: On request (new product, launched ~2025). Contact Bosch Engineering or marine integrators.
- Attractive for sterndrive/inboard adaptation on existing 2655.

## Battery Cost Context (2026 Market)
- LFP cells: ~$45–$60/kWh at cell level (China/utility scale); finished marine/C&I packs significantly higher ($150–400+/kWh installed depending on certification, cooling, BMS, marine rating).
- Evoy/RAD/Vision proprietary packs command premium for integration, certification, and support.
- Third-party high-voltage marine LFP packs possible but require careful matching (BMS, cooling, warranty implications).
- Target for 2655: 80–150+ kWh for mixed planing/displacement use with return capability.
