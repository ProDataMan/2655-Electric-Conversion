# Electric Party Barge / Pontoon Conversion Design Notes

**Goal**: Economical electric party barge to pair with the 2655 as the second vessel in a two-boat rental fleet, with designs that scale to additional units.

## Mission Profile

- Daytime party / cruising on Lake Union / Lake Washington (and similar protected waters).
- Moderate speeds sufficient for transit and positioning (planing optional but not required; efficient 6-12+ kt useful).
- High passenger capacity, open deck for seating, sound system, lighting, cooler/bar area.
- Quiet, zero-emission, easy docking.
- Solar top-up highly beneficial given large deck/bimini area.
- Fast shore charging for rental turnaround.

## Powertrain Preferences

- **Vision Marine E-Motion 180E + P Powerpack**: Strong existing pontoon integration (V24/V30 examples, 43-129 kWh packs). 180 hp class provides good performance on tri-toons. Turnkey-ish for pontoons.
- **RAD 40 or dual mid-power**: Compact, integrated steering, good efficiency. Suitable for moderate planing or efficient cruise.
- **ePropulsion X20/X40 or dual X-Series**: Mature, lower cost, excellent for displacement/party speeds. G-Series batteries.
- **Evoy Breeze or lower**: If higher performance desired.
- Twin motors preferred for maneuverability and redundancy in rental use.

## Hull / Platform Options

See `pontoon-options.md` for detailed research on used boats, bare frames, and kits.

Preferred characteristics:
- Aluminum pontoon tubes (or HDPE modular floats) for durability and low maintenance.
- 20-30+ ft length for party capacity.
- Tri-toon preferred for stability and higher speeds if planing is desired.
- Ample under-deck or dedicated battery space.
- Reinforced transom for outboard(s).

## Battery & Energy

- 40-100+ kWh LFP depending on range needs and solar contribution.
- Placement low and central for stability.
- Large solar array on bimini/roof practical (1-3+ kW potential).
- Shore charging (AC + DC where possible).

## Deck & Amenities for Rental

- Modular seating that can be configured for parties.
- Marine-grade sound system powered from house bank.
- LED lighting (RGB optional).
- Simple bar/cooler stations.
- Easy boarding (swim platforms, ladders).
- Clear range/SOC display and simple controls.

## Cost & Scalability Considerations

- Used pontoon acquisition + electric conversion often more economical than new electric pontoon.
- Bare frame / kit builds (Tiny Pontoon Boats / BigAssPontoonBoats style aluminum + HDPE or aluminum floats) allow customization and lower entry cost if welding/fabrication capability exists.
- Standardized powertrain and battery modules across fleet reduce spare parts and training.
- Local Seattle-area used market has options in the $10k-40k range for project boats.

## Next Steps

1. Identify 2-3 candidate used pontoons or bare platforms in the Seattle region.
2. Match powertrain shortlist to candidate hulls (weight, speed, cost).
3. Estimate conversion cost and timeline vs. purchasing a pre-built electric pontoon (e.g., Vision Marine examples).
4. Design modular battery/power distribution that can be reused across fleet vessels.

This design prioritizes low operating cost, high uptime, and guest experience for party/rental use.
