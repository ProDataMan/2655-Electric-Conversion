# Swedish Bayliner 2655 Electric Conversion — Anders Boström Reference

**Vessel**: 1990s-era Bayliner 2655 Ciera Sunbridge (identical hull class to the project boat).  
**Owner/Converter**: Anders Boström (Sweden).  
**Sources**: Skippo articles (2023-2024), Praktiskt Båtägande (2024), Maringuiden forum thread with link to personal site bostrom.name (as of 2023).

## Conversion Overview

- Removed original Mercruiser 5.0 LX V8 (~220 hp, ~450 kg).
- Installed **dual ePropulsion POD Drive 6.0 EVO** (6 kW each / ~9.9 hp equivalent, 48 V class).
- Custom battery bank: **32 cells totaling 28.67 kWh** (roughly equivalent to >3x ePropulsion E175 packs of ~8.96 kWh each). Chemistry not fully detailed in public reports but owner-sourced (likely LFP or similar).
- Removed sterndrive; pods mounted under the hull. Rudder initially fitted but found unnecessary due to dual-pod maneuverability.
- Resulting operation: **top speed ~6 knots**, **cruise ~4 knots**. Calculated range ~50-60 nm at 4 kt (estimates of ~15 hours runtime).

## Key Outcomes and Lessons

- **Weight**: Removing the heavy V8 and empty gas tank (~200 kg fuel equivalent) resulted in a net weight reduction despite battery addition (~200 kg batteries). Boat sat lighter aft initially; owner planned ballast/anchor windlass adjustments.
- **Performance**: Pure displacement mode. No planing capability. Quiet, vibration-free, odor-free engine room. Excellent for day cruising, low-speed operation, and maneuverability (dual independent pods).
- **Installation**: Challenging pod mounting through hull; heavy V8 removal was the biggest physical effort. Clean, modern installation once complete. Owner added electric steering assist, autopilot, new dashboard, extended swim platform, and anchor windlass.
- **Range & Efficiency**: At 4 kt, very efficient. Suitable for day trips and longer low-speed runs. Solar was discussed/planned in forums (e.g., flexible panels with Tenax mounts).
- **Philosophy**: Owner preferred pods over retaining sterndrive + inboard motor for dual-motor docking ease, elimination of bellows/impeller/maintenance, and space/cleanliness.

## Relevance to This Project

- **Proof of concept**: Demonstrates that a 2655 hull can be successfully converted to electric with pods and modest battery capacity for efficient displacement use.
- **Limitations for our goals**: 12 kW total is far below planing requirements. Our mixed-use (planing transit + displacement range) and rental needs demand significantly higher continuous power (80-150+ kW class) and larger battery capacity.
- **Positive takeaways**:
  - Engine room freed up for batteries/electronics.
  - Dual drives improve low-speed handling.
  - Weight savings from ICE + fuel removal provide budget for batteries.
  - Quiet operation ideal for rental and Lake Union/Washington use.
- **Adaptation ideas**: Higher-power pods (e.g., ePropulsion 12/20 kW eSSA or Helix-style compact units) or hybrid approach (high-power outboard/inboard + efficient secondary) could bridge the gap. Or use the Swedish approach as a low-cost baseline and add planing capability via a different primary drive.
