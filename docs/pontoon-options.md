# Pontoon Bare Frames, Used Barges & Economical Options for Electric Conversion

**Date**: November 2026  
**Focus**: Economical platforms suitable for electric party barge conversion in the Seattle / Pacific Northwest region, scalable for a rental fleet.

## Used Pontoon Boats (Seattle Area Snapshot)

Market examples (asking prices vary; verify current listings):
- Smaller/older models often $5k-15k.
- Mid-size 20-24 ft used (Bennington, Godfrey, Sun Tracker, etc.) commonly $20k-40k.
- Higher-end or recent 24-30 ft tri-toons $40k-80k+.
- Seattle median used pontoon asking around $8k range in broader data, but desirable party barges higher.
- Local sources: Craigslist Seattle, Boat Trader, Facebook Marketplace, dealers (Kenmore, Woodinville, etc.).

**Strategy**: Target project boats with sound pontoon logs/frames but tired gas engines or interiors. Remove ICE, add electric + batteries + refreshed deck. Weight savings from fuel/engine help offset battery mass.

## Bare Frames & DIY Kits

### Tinypontoonboats.com / BigAssPontoonBoats.com (NH-based, ships)
- Bolt-together aluminum frame systems with foam-filled HDPE floats (18" or larger 36" for big barges).
- Recreational mini kits (6x11 to larger) from ~$6k for frame+floats; larger systems higher.
- Large barge systems with 36" HDPE floats; aluminum or hybrid steel options. Capacities in the tens of thousands of pounds for big platforms.
- DIY aluminum I-beam concepts for West Coast builders to reduce freight.
- Transoms, decking, accessories available. Explicitly compatible with electric outboards (ePropulsion OEM partnership noted).
- Pros: Custom size, durable HDPE floats (unsinkable, corrosion-resistant), bolt-together reduces welding need for many builds.
- Cons: Freight cost to Washington; still requires assembly, decking, and finishing.

### Other Modular Systems
- Pond King and similar aluminum float kits (smaller scale).
- International options (UK Floats and Frames with Cutlass HDPE + aluminum) for concepts, though shipping heavy.
- Local aluminum fabricators in Pacific Northwest for custom frames using standard extrusions + purchased floats.

### Aluminum Tube Pontoons
- Traditional welded or modular aluminum logs. Many used boats already have these. New tubes available from suppliers.

## Economical Conversion Paths

1. **Used party barge + electric repower** (lowest barrier if sound hull found).
2. **Bare kit (Tiny Pontoon / similar) + custom deck + electric** — full control over layout and battery placement.
3. **Commercial paddle pub / work barge style platforms** already being converted (examples with ePropulsion X20 + 40 kWh packs for slow commercial use).
4. **Vision Marine or similar pre-integrated electric pontoons** — higher upfront cost but faster to revenue and proven systems.

## Key Considerations for Electric

- Transom strength for outboard thrust.
- Battery weight low and distributed (pontoons are stable but CG matters).
- Solar mounting area on bimini or hard top.
- Corrosion resistance (freshwater primary, but salt exposure possible via locks).
- Capacity ratings after adding batteries and passengers.
- Trailer compatibility for maintenance or seasonal moves.

## Seattle-Specific Notes

- Strong local used market and marine services.
- Protected waters favor efficient electric operation.
- Potential partnerships with local fabricators or electric marine specialists (e.g., Electrified Marine style installers).
