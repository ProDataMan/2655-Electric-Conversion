# 2655 Electric Conversion

Design documents, research, and plans for converting a 1995 Bayliner 2655 Ciera (sterndrive planing hull) to electric propulsion, plus party barge/pontoon electric conversions to build a rental fleet starting with two vessels and scaling to 10+.

## Project Goals

- Convert existing 1995 Bayliner 2655 (hole in gas tank, functional gas motor) into an electric boat suitable for mixed use: quick planing runs on Lake Union/Washington (520 bridge to I-90/Andrews Bay) and efficient displacement cruising for locks-to-Sound fishing and island hopping.
- Pair with an electric party barge / pontoon conversion for rental customers.
- Build a scalable rental fleet of at least 10 electric boats/party barges if the initial two prove successful.
- Prioritize reliability, maintainability, quiet operation, and reasonable trip times for rental customers while gaining range and zero-emission benefits.

## Key Use Cases

- **Planing mode**: Fast transit (~18-25 kt target) for party runs, return after full-day use.
- **Displacement mode**: Efficient 4-8 kt cruising for longer range, fishing, island hopping.
- Solar top-up while anchored/tied up.
- Rental operation: easy charging, low maintenance, high uptime.
